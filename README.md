### 🎯 Purpose

*I created this repository as a reflection of my journey through the ever-evolving landscape of Machine Learning. When I first embarked on this journey, I found myself struggling to navigate the vast sea of research papers. With countless papers to choose from, it was often difficult to distinguish the truly worthwhile from the merely relevant, let alone figure out where to begin.*

*This repository is, in many ways, a reflection of those struggles. Having experienced firsthand how overwhelming the learning curve can be, I hope to pay it forward by curating and organizing research papers that can serve as a useful starting point for beginners.*

*If this collection can save someone from the same rabbit hole of uncertainty I once found myself in, then it has served its purpose.*

### 🤝 Contributing

Everyone is welcome to contribute to this repository. If you know of a valuable research paper that deserves a place in this collection, feel free to contribute by opening a pull request.
